Attack

Deployed guards were attacked directly.

Eight independent Sepolia transactions tested repeated opening, duplicate settlement, post-open entry, locked configuration, unauthorized pool/reserve access, and claim replay. 8/8 mined with the expected status 0.

Fix

Exact encrypted selection replaced fixed slots.

Bounded rejection sampling selects uniformly over the encrypted total-weight interval. See the mathematical design.
